How it works

From eligibility to servicing, every file gets human review.

FORZA is designed as a narrow NJ pilot with manual underwriting, counsel-reviewed offer language, and no sensitive document collection on the public site.

    01

    Eligibility form

    The lead shares business basics: location, revenue band, time in business, desired funding, existing positions, and use of funds.

    02

    Funding call

    FORZA confirms fit, explains that eligibility is not approval, and outlines documents needed through a secure process.

    03

    Manual underwriting

    Review covers revenue, balances, negative days, NSFs, current positions, entity status, liens, owner identity, and use of funds.

    04

    Offer and disclosure

    Eligible merchants receive written terms and factor-rate math subject to counsel-reviewed documentation.

    05

    Funding and servicing

    Remittance starts with revenue percentage or weekly ACH with real reconciliation. Daily fixed ACH is reserved for higher-risk files.
