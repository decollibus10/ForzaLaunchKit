Disclosures

Important funding disclosures.

This page should be reviewed by counsel before launch and updated as New Jersey commercial financing rules evolve.

Business funding only

FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC is planning business receivables purchase funding for New Jersey businesses. The website does not offer consumer loans or personal-purpose financing.

Eligibility is not approval

Submitting an eligibility form does not guarantee approval, create a funding commitment, or obligate FORZA to make an offer. Final terms depend on underwriting, documentation, verification, and signed agreements.

Receivables purchase positioning

FORZA's intended funding product is a purchase of future business receivables. Public copy may reference merchant cash advance or MCA for education and search visibility, but final agreements should preserve the actual receivables-purchase structure.

Factor-rate examples

Example pricing may show a starting 1.50 factor rate and an eligible 30-day discount to a 1.10 total purchased amount. Examples are illustrative and are not promises of availability, approval, total cost, or repayment timing.

Reconciliation

Revenue-based funding should include a clear reconciliation process so remittance can be reviewed against actual business revenue when required by the agreement.

No confession of judgment

The planned contract posture avoids confession-of-judgment provisions. New Jersey restricts confession-of-judgment provisions in business financing contracts, including cash advances.

Counsel review required

Contracts, disclosures, advertisements, privacy language, servicing processes, and collection practices should be reviewed by qualified counsel before FORZA funds any deal.
