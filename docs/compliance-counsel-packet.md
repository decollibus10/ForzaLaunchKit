## NJ-Specific Issues To Review

### Confession Of Judgment

New Jersey restricts confession-of-judgment provisions in business financing contracts. The statute defines business financing broadly to include a loan, line of credit, cash advance, factoring, or asset-based transaction made for a business purpose.

FORZA operating rule:

- Do not use confession-of-judgment language.
- Do not use cognovit-note language.
- Require counsel signoff that every agreement version avoids this posture.

### Pending Commercial Financing Disclosure Bills

NJ S1760 and A4580 would require disclosures by providers of commercial financing if enacted. The introduced text covers commercial financing categories including sales-based financing and factoring transactions, and contemplates disclosures such as finance charge, APR or estimated yearly total dollar cost depending on product type, total payment amount, fees, and security interests.

FORZA operating rule:

- Build disclosure-ready even before enactment.
- Maintain a one-page offer summary with total purchased amount, factor rate, discount terms, fees, collateral/security interests, remittance method, and reconciliation process.
- Ask counsel to monitor effective dates, final regulations, and whether FORZA must make APR, estimated yearly total dollar cost, or other state-specific disclosures.

### Business Purpose

FORZA should stay business-purpose only at launch.

FORZA operating rule:

- No personal, family, household, consumer, or wage/paycheck advance use.
- Form and call script should ask for business use of funds.
- If use of funds appears personal or unclear, stop and send to manual review or decline.

## FTC Enforcement Pattern Controls

FTC merchant cash advance enforcement actions repeatedly focus on deception, unauthorized withdrawals, overcollection, misrepresented contract terms, abusive collection practices, and confession-of-judgment abuse.

FORZA operating controls:

- Do not advertise guaranteed approval, no-doc funding, free money, no risk, or misleading funding amounts.
- Do not claim there is no guaranty or collateral if the agreement contains any guaranty, security interest, UCC filing, or similar protection.
- Show deductions and fees clearly before signing.
- Stop debits when the purchased amount is satisfied.
- Maintain payoff/satisfaction controls.
- Provide reconciliation instructions in writing.
- Do not threaten, shame, harass, or use abusive collection tactics.
- Keep collection communications factual and documented.
- Provide refunds promptly if overcollection occurs.

## Data Security And Privacy

FORZA plans to collect sensitive business and owner information after a human review. That creates privacy and security obligations even if the public website form is intentionally light.

Counsel and security review should cover:

- Whether GLBA, FTC Safeguards Rule, Financial Privacy Rule, state privacy/security, or contract-based data-protection duties apply.
- Written information security program requirements.
- Secure document upload vendor.
- CRM limits and access permissions.
- Encryption, MFA, device security, and least-privilege access.
- Vendor contracts and service-provider safeguards.
- Retention and deletion schedule.
- Incident response and breach notification workflow.

FORZA operating rule:

- Do not accept bank statements, IDs, tax returns, contracts, SSNs, or bank logins through the public website form.
- Do not store raw sensitive documents in CRM notes.
- Store sensitive documents only in the approved secure document system.

## Advertising Review

Meta prohibits ads for financial products/services frequently associated with misleading or deceptive promotional practices and lists prohibited categories including short-term loans of 90 days or less. Because FORZA is positioning a business receivables purchase and not a loan, counsel should review the campaign setup, landing page, and language before spend.

FORZA operating rule:

- Use Meta financial-products/services review settings where applicable.
- Use broad NJ targeting.
- Avoid "loan" wording for the receivables-purchase product.
- Avoid claims based on distress or personal hardship.
- Avoid approval promises.
- Route ads to educational eligibility pages, not document upload.
