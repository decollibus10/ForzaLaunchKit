# NJ Funding Readiness Checklist

Use this as the checklist content behind `/resources/nj-funding-readiness-checklist/`.

## Before You Request Funding

- Confirm the business is based in New Jersey.
- Confirm the business has operated for at least 12 months.
- Estimate monthly gross revenue from recent deposits.
- Write down the exact use of funds.
- List current business loans, advances, vendor debt, tax issues, and UCC filings.
- Know whether any existing funding has a balance or payoff requirement.
- Review whether remittance would leave room for payroll, rent, taxes, and vendors.

## Questions To Ask Any Funder

- Is this a loan or a purchase of future receivables?
- What is the advance amount?
- What is the factor rate?
- What is the total purchased amount?
- Are there any fees or deductions?
- Is there a prepayment or early satisfaction discount?
- How is remittance collected?
- Can remittance be reconciled against actual revenue?
- What happens if sales drop?
- Is there a confession-of-judgment clause?

## Documents To Expect After A Call

Do not send sensitive documents through an unsecured public form. After a call, a funder may ask for:

- Recent business bank statements.
- Merchant processing statements or sales reports.
- Existing funding contracts and payoff balances.
- Entity status or formation details.
- Owner identity verification.
- UCC or tax lien clarification.
- Use-of-funds explanation.

## Red Flags

- Guaranteed approval.
- Pressure to sign before reading the agreement.
- No clear total purchased amount.
- No reconciliation process.
- Requests for bank login credentials through an unsecured public form.
- Personal-purpose financing presented as business funding.
- Multiple active advances without a realistic cash-flow plan.

## FORZA CTA

If your New Jersey business has 12+ months in business, $15k+ monthly gross revenue, and is seeking $5k-$15k for a business purpose, start with the short eligibility form.
