# Counsel Review Questions

Use this as the agenda for the first legal review call.

## Product Structure

1. Does the planned agreement preserve a true receivables-purchase / sales-based financing structure?
2. What facts or terms could cause the product to be treated as a loan?
3. Should the agreement use revenue percentage only for the first deals, or is weekly ACH with reconciliation acceptable?
4. What reconciliation language is required to support the intended structure?
5. How should the eligible 30-day discount to 1.10 be documented?
6. Are any fees permitted, and if so, how must they be disclosed?

## New Jersey Requirements

1. Does FORZA need any license, registration, notice filing, or exemption before funding NJ businesses?
2. Does NJ law restrict any planned security interest, UCC filing, guaranty, ACH authorization, or collection practice?
3. What contract language is required to comply with NJ's confession-of-judgment restriction?
4. What is the current status of S1760 and A4580?
5. If a commercial financing disclosure bill is enacted, what disclosures will FORZA need for sales-based financing or factoring?
6. Should FORZA voluntarily provide a disclosure summary before any new NJ law takes effect?

## Advertising And Sales

1. Is the website copy accurate and not misleading?
2. Can FORZA publicly state "offers may start at a 1.50 factor rate"?
3. Can FORZA describe the eligible 30-day discount to 1.10 in public copy?
4. What disclaimers should appear near pricing examples?
5. Which words should sales reps avoid on calls?
6. Should Meta ads say "funding," "receivables purchase," "MCA," or something else?

## Underwriting And Decisions

1. What anti-discrimination or fair-treatment rules should apply to business-purpose underwriting?
2. What documentation should FORZA keep for declines?
3. What must be included in the approval memo?
4. What records should be retained, and for how long?
5. What information should not be stored in the CRM?

## Privacy And Data Security

1. Does GLBA apply to FORZA's planned business model?
2. Does the FTC Safeguards Rule apply?
3. Does FORZA need a written information security program before requesting bank statements and IDs?
4. Which privacy notice should be given, and when?
5. What vendor-contract language is needed for document upload, CRM, email, hosting, and e-signature providers?
6. What is the breach notification workflow?

## Servicing And Collections

1. What ACH authorization language is required?
2. How should FORZA prevent overcollection after the purchased amount is satisfied?
3. What refund timing should be promised if overcollection occurs?
4. What collection language and practices are prohibited?
5. What should happen if the merchant closes, sells, or materially changes the business?
6. What events create breach/default without converting the transaction into a loan-like structure?

## Expansion

1. What states should FORZA avoid until state-specific disclosure and registration review is complete?
2. Which states require commercial financing disclosures, sales-based financing registration, or broker disclosures?
3. What changes are required before accepting out-of-state applications?
