# FORZA Meta Ad Launch Kit

Budget assumption: under $1,000/month for the first test.

## Policy Setup

- Use Meta's **Financial products and services** Special Ad Category for campaigns promoting business funding.
- Expect limited audience tools and avoid narrow targeting that could create discrimination risk.
- Do not use ZIP-code targeting, age/gender filtering, or sensitive financial-condition targeting.
- Use broad New Jersey location targeting and let creative plus landing-page qualification do the filtering.
- Send funding ads to the eligibility page, not directly to a document-upload flow.

Meta source notes:

- Meta announced that Financial products and services replaced the previous Credit ads category and became required for U.S. financial products/services campaigns starting January 21, 2025.
- Meta's prohibited financial-products policy says ads must not promote financial products or services associated with misleading or deceptive promotional practices.

## Campaign Structure

| Campaign | Objective | Audience | Budget | Landing Page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NJ Funding Eligibility Test | Leads or landing-page views | Broad New Jersey, Special Ad Category enabled | $20-$30/day max | `/nj-business-funding/` or `/eligibility/` |
| NJ Checklist Lead Magnet | Leads or landing-page views | Broad New Jersey, Special Ad Category enabled | $10-$20/day max | `/resources/nj-funding-readiness-checklist/` |
| AI Audit Test | Leads or landing-page views | Broad New Jersey business operators | $10-$15/day max | `/ai-automation-audit/` |

Start with funding eligibility and checklist lead magnet only for 10-14 days. Add the AI audit campaign after the funding page, HubSpot routing, and follow-up process are stable.

## Creative Directions

1. **Receivables clarity**
   - Visual: service-business desk, invoices, tablet, payment terminal.
   - Hook: explain that funding is reviewed from business receivables.

2. **Eligibility education**
   - Visual: clean checklist.
   - Hook: NJ businesses can check basic fit before sharing sensitive documents.

3. **Transparent example**
   - Visual: simple math panel with advance, factor, total purchased amount.
   - Hook: see example structure before a call.

4. **Readiness checklist**
   - Visual: clean pre-call checklist.
   - Hook: know what to review before comparing MCA or receivables-purchase offers.

## Do Not Use

- Guaranteed approval.
- Instant approval.
- No-doc funding.
- Bad credit approved.
- Everyone qualifies.
- No risk.
- Free money.
- No fees unless counsel approves exact claim.
- Loan language for the receivables-purchase product.
- Personal hardship targeting such as "struggling," "desperate," "behind on bills," or "can't make payroll."
