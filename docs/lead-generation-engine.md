# FORZA Lead Generation Engine

Goal: make lead generation a central part of the launch, with organic SEO, focused landing pages, HubSpot routing, and conservative compliance controls.

## Funnel Architecture

Primary funding path:

```text
SEO / Meta / referral
-> landing page
-> short eligibility form
-> HubSpot lead record
-> funding call
-> secure document request
-> manual underwriting
-> counsel-reviewed offer
```

Secondary nurture path:

```text
SEO / social / retargeting
-> NJ Funding Readiness Checklist
-> HubSpot resource lead
-> education email
-> eligibility CTA
```

Separate consulting path:

```text
AI content / referral
-> AI Automation Audit page
-> HubSpot AI audit lead
-> consulting call
```

## Static Pages Built For Leads

- `/` - brand homepage and broad CTA.
- `/nj-business-funding/` - statewide NJ funding hub for SEO and paid campaigns.
- `/eligibility/` - primary conversion page.
- `/industries/contractor-funding-nj/` - contractor and trades search intent.
- `/industries/restaurant-funding-nj/` - restaurant and food service search intent.
- `/industries/salon-funding-nj/` - salon and wellness search intent.
- `/industries/auto-repair-funding-nj/` - auto repair and service-shop search intent.
- `/resources/nj-funding-readiness-checklist/` - lead magnet for research-stage owners.
- `/insights/` - article hub for weekly SEO publishing.
- `/ai-automation-audit/` - separate consulting lead path.

## Lead Quality Rules

High-priority funding lead:

- New Jersey business.
- 12+ months in business.
- $15k+ monthly gross revenue.
- $5k-$15k funding request.
- No active advance, or one top-tier senior position that can be reviewed.
- Specific use of funds.

Manual-review lead:

- Existing funding position.
- Requested amount above pilot range.
- Unclear industry or use of funds.
- Resource lead with strong revenue but no funding request yet.

Nurture or decline:

- Outside NJ.
- Under 12 months in business.
- Under $15k monthly revenue.
- Multiple active advances.
- Consumer/personal-purpose request.

## Weekly SEO Publishing Themes

Use one article per week, then internally link each article to a relevant conversion page.

- MCA basics in New Jersey.
- Revenue-based funding vs. loans.
- Factor rates explained.
- Prepayment discount examples.
- Reconciliation in revenue-based funding.
- Second-position funding risks.
- Contractor funding in NJ.
- Restaurant working-capital timing.
- Salon cash-flow planning.
- Auto repair shop funding use cases.
- What documents are requested after a funding call.
- When not to use an MCA.

## Paid Lead Test

Meta test budget: under $1k/month.

Campaign structure:

- Campaign 1: Funding eligibility, NJ statewide, broad service-business messaging.
- Campaign 2: Checklist lead magnet, NJ statewide, education-first messaging.
- Campaign 3: Retarget site visitors after privacy/counsel review.

Do not use claims like guaranteed approval, no credit check, no hidden fees, instant funding, or best rates.

## HubSpot Follow-Up SLAs

- Funding eligibility lead: call or email within 1 business hour during launch hours.
- Resource lead: send checklist immediately, then follow up within 1 business day if pilot fit is visible.
- AI audit lead: respond within 1 business day.
- Outside-NJ lead: send polite not-available-yet response and tag for expansion.

## Metrics To Watch Weekly

- Visits by landing page.
- Eligibility form conversion rate.
- Checklist conversion rate.
- Qualified lead rate.
- Call booked rate.
- Docs requested rate.
- Offer sent rate.
- Funded rate.
- Decline reasons by source.
- Cost per lead and cost per qualified lead for Meta.

## Compliance Guardrails

- Keep funding and AI consulting separate.
- Keep forms limited to business basics.
- Keep examples clearly marked as illustrative and subject to underwriting/final agreement.
- Route outside-NJ leads away from active funding offers.
- Keep noindex enabled until counsel/content review is complete.
