# Payoff And Satisfaction Letter Templates

Counsel draft only. Use after agreement terms and servicing process are approved.

## Payoff / Satisfaction Quote

Subject:

> FORZA satisfaction quote for `[BUSINESS NAME]`

Body:

> This satisfaction quote is provided by FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC for the business receivables purchase agreement dated `[DATE]`.
>
> Business: `[BUSINESS LEGAL NAME]`
>
> File ID: `[FILE ID]`
>
> Quote date: `[DATE]`
>
> Quote valid through: `[DATE/TIME]`
>
> Purchased receivables amount: `$[PURCHASED_AMOUNT]`
>
> Remittances received and applied: `$[RECEIVED]`
>
> Remaining satisfaction amount: `$[BALANCE]`
>
> Eligible discount amount, if applicable: `$[DISCOUNT_BALANCE]`
>
> Payment instructions: `[APPROVED PAYMENT METHOD]`
>
> This quote is subject to the signed agreement and receipt of good funds by the deadline above.

## Paid / Satisfied Confirmation

Subject:

> FORZA agreement satisfied - `[BUSINESS NAME]`

Body:

> FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC confirms that the purchased receivables amount under the business receivables purchase agreement dated `[DATE]` for `[BUSINESS LEGAL NAME]` has been satisfied as of `[DATE]`.
>
> File ID: `[FILE ID]`
>
> Total purchased receivables amount: `$[PURCHASED_AMOUNT]`
>
> Total received and applied: `$[RECEIVED]`
>
> FORZA will update its servicing records accordingly. If a UCC filing or other public record was filed and counsel-approved release is required, FORZA will process the release according to the agreement and applicable procedure.

## Overcollection Review Notice

Subject:

> FORZA overcollection review - `[BUSINESS NAME]`

Body:

> FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC identified or received notice of a possible overcollection on file `[FILE ID]`.
>
> Amount under review: `$[AMOUNT]`
>
> Date identified: `[DATE]`
>
> FORZA will review the account history and agreement terms. If an overcollection is confirmed, FORZA will issue any required credit or refund according to the agreement and approved servicing procedure.

## Counsel Notes

- Confirm whether "payoff" or "satisfaction" is the preferred term.
- Confirm UCC release timing.
- Confirm refund timing and method.
- Confirm whether any release language could waive unrelated rights.
