# FORZA Legal Template Pack

These are counsel-draft templates for FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC. They are not final legal documents and should not be sent to merchants, signed, or used to fund a deal until qualified counsel approves the exact forms.

## Included Drafts

- `offer-summary-disclosure-template.md`
- `business-receivables-purchase-agreement-counsel-draft.md`
- `performance-guaranty-counsel-draft.md`
- `ach-authorization-counsel-draft.md`
- `reconciliation-request-form.md`
- `payoff-and-satisfaction-letters.md`
- `servicing-and-collections-sop.md`
- `merchant-call-scripts.md`

## Drafting Assumptions

- NJ-only launch.
- Business-purpose transactions only.
- Product is intended as a future receivables purchase / sales-based financing structure.
- First pilot offers are $5,000-$15,000.
- Public examples may reference a 1.50 factor rate and eligible 30-day discount to 1.10, subject to underwriting and agreement.
- Performance guaranty posture only unless counsel changes it.
- No confession-of-judgment or cognovit-note posture.

## Counsel Must Resolve

- Whether the final product is properly characterized and serviced as a receivables purchase.
- Whether any NJ license, registration, disclosure, privacy, or servicing requirement applies before launch.
- Whether pending NJ commercial financing disclosure bills require changes if enacted.
- Exact reconciliation language.
- Exact ACH authorization and revocation language.
- Exact permitted collection practices.
- Retention, privacy, and secure document procedures.
