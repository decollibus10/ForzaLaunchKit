## Guaranteed Performance Obligations

Counsel to draft whether Guarantor guarantees only performance obligations such as:

- Truthful information.
- No fraud or intentional misrepresentation.
- No diversion of receivables.
- Cooperation with reconciliation and servicing records.
- Notice of material business changes.
- No unauthorized change of bank account intended to impair remittance.
- No undisclosed stacking or additional financing in violation of agreement terms.
