# Servicing And Collections SOP

Counsel draft only. This SOP must be reviewed before any merchant is funded.

## Servicing Principles

- Keep communications factual and professional.
- Do not threaten, shame, harass, or misrepresent consequences.
- Do not debit more than the agreement permits.
- Stop scheduled remittance when the purchased amount is satisfied.
- Review reconciliation requests according to the agreement.
- Escalate legal questions to counsel.

## Active Servicing Checklist

Weekly:

- Confirm expected remittance received.
- Update remaining purchased receivables balance.
- Check for overcollection risk.
- Review failed debits or reversals.
- Review merchant communications.
- Confirm any reconciliation request status.

Monthly:

- Review active exposure.
- Review merchant concentration.
- Review second-position files.
- Review missed remittance patterns.
- Review complaints.

## Missed Remittance Workflow

1. Confirm whether the debit failed, was returned, or was not initiated.
2. Check whether a reconciliation request is pending.
3. Contact merchant using approved script.
4. Ask for factual explanation and expected resolution.
5. Document in CRM.
6. If issue persists, escalate to manual review.
7. Do not make legal threats unless counsel approves exact language.

## Reconciliation Workflow

1. Receive request.
2. Confirm request is complete under the agreement.
3. Request missing documents if incomplete.
4. Review actual revenue for the stated period.
5. Calculate adjustment according to the agreement.
6. Send decision notice.
7. Update servicing schedule.
8. Save request, documents, calculation, and decision notice.

## Overcollection Workflow

1. Stop additional debits if satisfaction appears complete.
2. Reconcile total purchased amount against received remittances.
3. Confirm whether any pending ACH has not settled.
4. If overcollection is confirmed, process refund or credit according to counsel-approved timing.
5. Send overcollection review notice or refund confirmation.
6. Document file.

## Collections Guardrails

Do not:

- Claim the merchant committed a crime without counsel approval.
- Contact unrelated third parties except as counsel approves.
- Misrepresent legal rights.
- Misrepresent amount owed or purchased amount remaining.
- Continue debiting after satisfaction.
- Use confession-of-judgment language.
- Threaten actions FORZA does not intend or cannot legally take.

## Complaint Workflow

1. Acknowledge complaint.
2. Stop argumentative communication.
3. Assign owner.
4. Preserve records.
5. Review agreement, call notes, ads, and servicing logs.
6. Escalate to counsel when complaint involves legal rights, privacy, debits, collections, or misrepresentation.
7. Send written response only after review.

## Counsel Notes

- Confirm all communications comply with applicable federal and state laws.
- Confirm whether any debt-collection, commercial collection, or servicing rules apply.
- Confirm whether refund timing should be expressed in business days.
