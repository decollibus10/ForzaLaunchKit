# Merchant Call Scripts

Counsel and compliance draft only. Train callers to use consistent language and avoid promises.

## Opening

> Thanks for speaking with FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC. Before we discuss details, I want to be clear that this call is for business-purpose funding review only. This is not approval and not a commitment to fund. If the basics fit, we can explain what documents would be needed for underwriting.

## Product Explanation

> FORZA's planned funding product is structured as a purchase of future business receivables. That means we review business revenue, existing obligations, and whether the business can support the remittance structure. It is not consumer financing and is not for personal, family, or household use.

## Eligibility Questions

- Is the business based in New Jersey?
- How long has the business been operating?
- What is the average monthly gross revenue?
- What is the requested funding amount?
- What is the business use of funds?
- Are there any active advances, loans, liens, or funding positions?
- Is the business in bankruptcy or subject to major litigation?

## Pricing Explanation

> Some FORZA examples show a factor rate. A factor rate is a multiplier used to calculate the total purchased receivables amount. For example, a $10,000 purchase price at a 1.50 factor rate equals a $15,000 purchased receivables amount. Final terms depend on underwriting and the signed agreement.

## Discount Explanation

> Some offers may include an eligible 30-day satisfaction discount. If available, the conditions will be written in the agreement. It should not be treated as automatic unless the signed agreement says the conditions are met.

## Reconciliation Explanation

> Reconciliation is the process for reviewing remittance against actual revenue. The final agreement should explain how to request it, what records are needed, and how adjustments are reviewed.

## Document Request

> The public website form does not collect sensitive documents. If we move to underwriting, please use the secure upload process. Do not email bank statements, IDs, tax documents, or contracts unless FORZA has specifically approved that process.

## If Merchant Asks "Am I Approved?"

> Not yet. At this stage we can only say whether the file appears to fit the basic pilot criteria. Approval requires document review, underwriting, and final agreement approval.

## If Merchant Asks "Is This A Loan?"

> FORZA is planning a business receivables-purchase structure. The final agreement controls the exact terms. You should review it with your own legal, tax, accounting, or financial advisors.

## If Merchant Is Outside NJ

> FORZA is currently focused on New Jersey businesses only. We are not requesting documents or quoting terms for out-of-state businesses at this time.

## Do Not Say

- You are approved.
- Everyone qualifies.
- Guaranteed funding.
- No documents needed.
- Bad credit approved.
- No risk.
- No fees unless counsel has approved the exact statement.
- This is free money.
- This is just like a loan.
- We can fund regardless of revenue.
