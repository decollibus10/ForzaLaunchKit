## 4. Remittance Method

Recipient will remit the specified percentage of receivables or the estimated remittance amount through the method stated in the final agreement.

Possible method:

- Revenue percentage.
- Weekly ACH with reconciliation.
- Daily ACH with reconciliation for higher-risk files only.

Counsel drafting note: select one method per agreement and remove unused alternatives.

## 5. Reconciliation

Recipient may request reconciliation if actual revenue materially differs from the assumptions used to set remittance.

The final agreement should specify:

- How to request reconciliation.
- What records must be provided.
- Time period reviewed.
- How adjustments are calculated.
- When adjustments become effective.
- Whether adjustments can move up or down.
- How disputes are handled.

Draft placeholder:

> If Recipient believes the remittance amount is not aligned with actual receivables for the applicable period, Recipient may request reconciliation by `[METHOD]`. Recipient must provide `[DOCUMENTS]` for `[PERIOD]`. FORZA will review the request within `[NUMBER] business days` after receiving complete documentation and will make any adjustment required by this Agreement.

## 6. Eligible Early Satisfaction Discount

If approved for the transaction, the agreement may include an eligible 30-day discount.

Draft placeholder:

> If Recipient satisfies all conditions in this Agreement and elects to satisfy the purchased receivables amount within 30 calendar days after funding, FORZA may accept `$[DISCOUNT_AMOUNT]`, equal to `[DISCOUNT_FACTOR]` times the purchase price, as full satisfaction of the purchased receivables amount.

Counsel drafting note: define exact conditions, deadline, payment timing, exclusions, and whether the discount is optional or mandatory.

## 7. Recipient Covenants

Recipient agrees to:

- Provide truthful information.
- Maintain the business bank account unless FORZA is notified as required by the agreement.
- Not divert receivables in violation of the agreement.
- Not take additional financing that impairs FORZA's purchased receivables without required notice or consent, if counsel approves this covenant.
- Provide records reasonably required for reconciliation and servicing.
- Notify FORZA of material changes to the business.

## 8. Events Of Breach

Potential events for counsel review:

- Material misrepresentation.
- Fraud.
- Diversion of receivables.
- Blocking agreed ACH remittance without following reconciliation or dispute procedure.
- Closing or selling the business without required notice.
- Taking additional advances in violation of agreement terms.
- Refusing to provide required reconciliation or servicing records.

Counsel drafting note: avoid breach language that contradicts the intended receivables-purchase characterization.

## 9. Performance Guaranty

If required, the agreement should reference a separate performance guaranty.

Counsel drafting note: confirm guaranty scope does not become an impermissible or inconsistent payment guaranty unless counsel intentionally drafts it that way.

## 10. ACH Authorization

If ACH is used, attach a separate ACH authorization reviewed for NACHA, banking, privacy, revocation, and error-handling requirements.

## 11. No Confession Of Judgment

The agreement must not contain a confession-of-judgment clause, cognovit note, or similar provision.

## 12. Overcollection And Satisfaction

The agreement should require FORZA to maintain controls to stop remittance after satisfaction of the purchased amount and to address any overcollection promptly.

> FORZA will apply received remittances against the purchased receivables amount and will stop scheduled remittance when the purchased receivables amount has been satisfied. Any overcollection identified by FORZA or Recipient will be reviewed and addressed according to `[REFUND PROCESS]`.
