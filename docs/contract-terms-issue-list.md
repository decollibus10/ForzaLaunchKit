# Contract Terms Issue List

This is not a contract. It is a checklist for counsel to use when drafting or reviewing the FORZA agreement package.

## Core Agreement Terms

- Legal names of provider and recipient.
- Business-purpose representation.
- Purchase price / advance amount.
- Purchased receivables amount.
- Factor rate.
- Estimated term, if used.
- Remittance method.
- Revenue percentage or payment schedule.
- Reconciliation procedure.
- Fees, if any.
- Early satisfaction / 30-day discount terms.
- Merchant reporting obligations.
- Bank account maintenance obligations.
- No personal, family, or household use.

## Receivables-Purchase Characterization

- Clear sale of future receivables language.
- Economic risk tied to merchant revenue where appropriate.
- Reconciliation rights.
- No absolute repayment promise inconsistent with structure unless counsel approves.
- Treatment if business revenue drops.
- Treatment if business legitimately ceases operations.
- Treatment if merchant blocks reporting or diverts receivables.

## Guaranty And Security

- Performance guaranty scope.
- No broad payment guaranty unless counsel approves.
- UCC filing posture.
- Security interest description, if any.
- No confession-of-judgment clause.
- No cognovit note.
- No hidden collateral claim.

## ACH And Servicing

- ACH authorization.
- Debit frequency.
- Debit amount calculation.
- Reconciliation request mechanics.
- Pause or adjustment process.
- Overcollection prevention.
- Refund timing.
- Paid-in-full / satisfied agreement confirmation.

## Defaults, Breach, And Remedies

- Misrepresentation.
- Fraud.
- Diversion of receivables.
- Blocking ACH or changing bank accounts without notice.
- Undisclosed additional financing.
- Bankruptcy or insolvency events.
- Sale or closure of business.
- Cure periods.
- Notice requirements.
- Permitted remedies.
- Prohibited collection practices.

## Disclosures And Attachments

- One-page offer summary.
- Total purchased amount.
- Factor rate.
- Discount terms.
- Fees.
- Remittance method.
- Reconciliation instructions.
- Security interests or collateral requirements.
- Broker/referral compensation if any.
- Privacy notice.
- ACH authorization.
- Performance guaranty.

## Final Counsel Signoff

- NJ launch approved.
- Website copy approved.
- Ad copy approved.
- Agreement approved.
- Disclosures approved.
- Privacy/data security process approved.
- Servicing and reconciliation approved.
- Funding-day checklist approved.
