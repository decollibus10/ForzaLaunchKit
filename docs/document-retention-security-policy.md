# Document Retention And Security Policy Starter

This policy starter is for counsel and security review. It is not legal advice and should not be treated as final.

## Guiding Rule

Collect the least sensitive information needed at each stage, store it only in approved systems, restrict access, and delete it when the business no longer needs it and retention obligations allow deletion.

## Public Website Form

Allowed:

- Business legal name.
- Owner/contact name.
- Business email.
- Phone number.
- NJ location.
- Industry.
- Monthly revenue range.
- Time in business.
- Desired funding range.
- Existing funding status.
- Use of funds.
- Consent.

Not allowed:

- SSN.
- Bank login credentials.
- Bank statements.
- Tax returns.
- Driver's license images.
- Owner home address.
- Existing contracts.
- Full bank account numbers.
- Payment card numbers.

## Secure Document Collection

Use only after human review.

Potential documents:

- Business bank statements.
- Merchant processing statements.
- Existing funding contracts.
- Entity documents.
- Owner identity verification through approved vendor/process.
- UCC/tax lien/litigation search results.

Requirements:

- MFA on document system.
- Least-privilege access.
- No sensitive document attachments in normal email.
- No sensitive documents pasted into CRM notes.
- Vendor contract reviewed for security and confidentiality.
- Access log retained where available.

## CRM Rules

Allowed CRM notes:

- Lead status.
- Call notes.
- Stated revenue range.
- Document request status.
- Underwriting summary.
- Offer status.
- Servicing status.
- Follow-up date.

Avoid CRM notes:

- Full bank account numbers.
- SSNs.
- Driver's license numbers.
- Raw bank statement screenshots.
- Owner home addresses unless counsel approves a legitimate need and storage process.
- Medical, family, or unrelated personal information.

## Retention Draft

Counsel should approve final retention periods.

| Record Type | Draft Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Declined leads with no documents | 12-24 months |
| Funded file agreement package | 7 years after satisfaction |
| Bank statements and underwriting support | 7 years after satisfaction or counsel-approved shorter period |
| Ad review records | 3 years |
| Approval memos | 7 years |
| Servicing and reconciliation logs | 7 years |
| Website form submissions | 24 months if not funded |

## Access Controls

- Use MFA on email, CRM, document storage, WordPress, domain registrar, hosting, and ad accounts.
- Use separate accounts for each team member.
- Remove access immediately when a person no longer works on FORZA matters.
- Review access monthly during launch.
- Do not share passwords by text, email, or chat.
- Use a password manager.

## Incident Response Starter

If sensitive data may be exposed:

1. Stop the exposure if possible.
2. Preserve evidence.
3. Identify affected systems and records.
4. Notify counsel immediately.
5. Follow counsel-approved notification and remediation steps.
6. Document timeline, decisions, and corrective actions.
