# Content Flywheel

One approved thesis should create one flagship memo, several channel-native assets, one conversion artifact, and the next research seed.

## Thesis To Asset Map

| Asset | Purpose | Required Inputs | Output Standard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Substack article | Trust and monetization | Thesis, evidence pack, skeptic review, claim register | Teaches judgment and preserves uncertainty |
| LinkedIn post | Professional reach and replies | Core thesis, one operator lesson, one concrete example | Native post with no generic CTA |
| X thread | Conversation and idea testing | Thesis, 4 to 8 claims, caveats | Short sequence with one useful takeaway per post |
| Paid subscriber teaser | Conversion | Free/premium split and strongest paid insight | Makes the paid section feel specific, not hidden |
| Lead magnet / memo | Durable capture asset | Checklist, framework, worksheet, or memo template | Useful without sales pressure |
| Next article seed | Flywheel continuation | Reader replies, objections, unaddressed evidence gaps | One clear follow-up question |

## Memo Conversion Logic

Every memo needs a conversion thesis:

```text
This piece should make the right reader believe:
1. The operator sees a pattern they do not see yet.
2. The pattern affects money, risk, or decision quality.
3. Paid membership gives them more of the operating framework, not more fluff.
```

## Distribution Sequence

| Timing | Action |
| --- | --- |
| T-1 day | Publish LinkedIn observation without link to test language |
| T day | Publish Substack memo |
| T day + 2 hours | Publish X thread with the strongest framework |
| T day + 1 day | Publish LinkedIn post with reader-action framing |
| T day + 2 days | Send paid teaser or note to free subscribers |
| T day + 3 days | Capture replies, objections, and questions as new seeds |

## Repurposing Rules

- Do not paste the same prose across channels.
- LinkedIn should foreground operator judgment and conversation.
- X should foreground the framework and sharp claims.
- Substack should carry the full evidence chain.
- Paid teaser should sell specificity, not secrecy.
- Lead magnets should be tools: checklists, memos, worksheets, source maps.

## Next Seed Extraction

After publication, create follow-up seeds from:

- The best reader objection.
- The most common misunderstanding.
- The strongest paid-conversion comment.
- The highest-save or highest-share angle.
- The unanswered evidence gap.
- The claim that generated the highest-quality inbound.
