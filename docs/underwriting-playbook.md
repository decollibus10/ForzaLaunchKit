# FORZA Underwriting Playbook

This is an operating template, not legal, tax, or credit advice. Counsel should review the final process before the first funded deal.

## Launch Box

- Geography: New Jersey only.
- Product: business receivables purchase / revenue-based funding.
- First advance size: $5,000-$15,000.
- Minimum time in business: 12+ months.
- Minimum monthly gross revenue: $15,000+.
- Position: first position preferred; second position only behind top-tier lenders with adequate cash flow.
- Security posture: performance guaranty only; no confession of judgment.

## Documents To Request After Human Review

- Last 3-6 months business bank statements.
- Merchant processing statements if applicable.
- Existing MCA/funding contracts and current balances.
- Entity formation or good standing evidence.
- Owner identity verification through approved secure process.
- UCC, tax lien, judgment, bankruptcy, and litigation search results.
- Proof of business address if needed.

## First-Pass Cash-Flow Review

| Check | Target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly gross revenue | $15k+ | Use actual deposits, not only stated revenue |
| Average daily balance | Positive and stable | Watch for end-of-month dips |
| Negative days | Low and explainable | Recurring negative days are a red flag |
| NSFs/returns | Minimal | Pattern matters more than one-off issue |
| Deposit consistency | Recurring business revenue | Exclude owner transfers where possible |
| Existing remittance load | Sustainable | Include senior lender/MCA payments |
| Use of funds | Business-purpose and specific | Avoid vague or personal-purpose needs |

## Position Review

### First Position

- Preferred for the pilot.
- Confirm no active receivables sale or senior ACH obligation.
- Confirm no undisclosed UCC filings that suggest active financing.

### Second Position

Review only if all are true:

- Senior funder is reputable and contract is available.
- Current balance and remittance are verified.
- Combined remittance leaves operating room for payroll, rent, taxes, vendors, and normal expenses.
- Merchant understands reconciliation and reporting obligations.
- No sign of stacking beyond the disclosed senior position.

## Offer Structure Checklist

- Advance amount.
- Factor rate.
- Total purchased receivables amount.
- Eligible 30-day prepayment discount amount, if offered.
- Estimated remittance method: revenue percentage, weekly ACH with reconciliation, or daily fixed ACH for higher-risk files.
- Reconciliation instructions.
- Fees, if any.
- Events of default or breach.
- Performance guaranty scope.
- No confession-of-judgment language.

## Risk Flags

- Business outside New Jersey.
- Less than 12 months in business.
- Under $15k monthly gross revenue.
- Multiple active advances or unclear payoff balances.
- Frequent negative days, NSFs, or returned payments.
- Tax liens, undisclosed UCCs, litigation, or bankruptcy concerns.
- Use of funds appears personal, speculative, or unclear.
- Merchant refuses existing contract review.
- Requested amount exceeds pilot box without strong compensating factors.

## Decline Language

Use neutral, factual language:

> Thank you for sharing your information. Based on the current FORZA pilot criteria, we are not able to move forward with an offer at this time. This decision is based on current business-fit and underwriting factors and is not a commitment to fund. You are welcome to reconnect if revenue, time in business, or existing obligations change.

## Funding-Day Controls

- Confirm agreement version is final and signed.
- Confirm business bank account ownership.
- Confirm servicing schedule and reconciliation path.
- Confirm CRM status is `Funded`.
- Confirm follow-up date is set.
- Save final approval memo before sending funds.
