# FORZA Follow-Up Templates

Use these as starting points. Keep all merchant communications factual and avoid promising approval.

## New Lead: Prequalified

Subject:

> FORZA eligibility review - next step

Body:

> Thanks for checking eligibility with FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC. Based on the basics you submitted, your business may fit our current NJ pilot criteria.
>
> The next step is a short call to understand your use of funds, current obligations, and whether a receivables-purchase structure makes sense. This is not approval or a commitment to fund.
>
> Please reply with a few times that work for you, or use the scheduling link once provided.

## Manual Review

Subject:

> FORZA funding review - additional context needed

Body:

> Thanks for reaching out to FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC. Your submission needs manual review because of the amount requested, existing funding position, or another file detail.
>
> A team member can review your use of funds and current obligations before requesting documents. No offer is available unless underwriting supports it and final terms are approved.

## Outside NJ

Subject:

> FORZA is currently focused on New Jersey

Body:

> Thanks for your interest in FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC. We are currently focused on New Jersey businesses only.
>
> We can keep your inquiry on file for future expansion, but we are not reviewing out-of-state funding requests at this time.

## Not Prequalified Yet

Subject:

> FORZA eligibility review

Body:

> Thank you for sharing your information. Based on the current FORZA pilot criteria, your business may not fit the first funding program yet.
>
> The initial pilot is focused on New Jersey businesses with 12+ months in business, $15k+ monthly gross revenue, and first funding requests of $5k-$15k. You are welcome to reconnect if those factors change.

## Secure Docs Request

Subject:

> Secure document request for FORZA review

Body:

> Thank you for speaking with FORZA CAPITAL PARTNERS LLC. To continue underwriting, please use the secure upload link provided separately.
>
> Please do not email bank statements, IDs, tax documents, or contracts as regular attachments unless FORZA has approved that process. The public website form is intentionally limited to business basics.
>
> Requested items:
>
> - Last 3-6 months business bank statements.
> - Existing funding contracts and current balances, if any.
> - Entity documentation or good standing evidence.
> - Merchant processing statements, if applicable.
>
> Receiving documents does not guarantee approval or create a funding commitment.
