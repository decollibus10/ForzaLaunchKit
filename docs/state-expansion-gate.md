# State Expansion Gate

FORZA is NJ-only at launch. Do not accept, underwrite, advertise to, or fund out-of-state businesses until counsel clears the state.

## Expansion Rule

Before entering a new state, complete:

- Commercial financing disclosure review.
- Sales-based financing / MCA registration review.
- Broker/referral compensation review.
- Lending, usury, licensing, and loan-characterization review.
- Confession-of-judgment and collection-practice review.
- Privacy/security and document-retention review.
- Ad targeting and landing-page review.
- Agreement choice-of-law and venue review.
- State-specific disclosure template.
- CRM routing rule for that state.

## Website Routing

Until approved:

- Public eligibility form should route non-NJ businesses to `outside_nj`.
- Sales team should not request documents from non-NJ businesses.
- Ads should target New Jersey only.
- SEO content may be educational, but CTAs should make NJ-only availability clear.

## Operational Lock

If a non-NJ lead enters CRM:

1. Mark `outside_nj`.
2. Send outside-NJ template.
3. Do not request documents.
4. Do not quote terms.
5. Do not refer to a broker or funder unless counsel approves broker/referral process.
