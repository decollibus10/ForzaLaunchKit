# FORZA Funding-Day Checklist

Use this only after counsel-approved documents are ready and the approval memo is complete.

## Before Sending Final Agreement

- Approval memo complete.
- Deal Desk calculation saved or printed.
- Merchant legal name confirmed.
- Entity status checked.
- Owner identity verified through approved process.
- Business bank account ownership confirmed.
- Bank statements reviewed.
- Existing contracts and payoff balances reviewed.
- UCC/tax lien/litigation concerns resolved or documented.
- Use of funds confirmed as business-purpose.
- Reconciliation process included in agreement.
- Performance guaranty scope reviewed.
- No confession-of-judgment language confirmed.

## Before Sending Funds

- Final signed agreement saved.
- Final purchased amount confirmed.
- Remittance method confirmed.
- Discount terms, if any, confirmed in writing.
- Servicing schedule created.
- Merchant has reconciliation instructions.
- CRM status changed to `Funded`.
- First follow-up date set.
- Funding amount matches approval memo.
- Wire/ACH destination verified through approved process.

## After Funding

- Save confirmation of funds sent.
- Create servicing record.
- Add file to portfolio exposure tracker.
- Schedule first servicing review.
- Monitor first remittance.
- Watch early warning indicators: missed payment, unexpected negative balance, new UCC, new advance, unreachable owner, or sudden revenue drop.
